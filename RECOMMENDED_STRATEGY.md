# Choose the HER2 experiments that the evidence can support

**Decision.** Enter the trastuzumab optimisation track with the existing paired Fab-variable designs, subject to the organiser's track assignment. Lead the pitch with the assay-transfer experiment. Use matched geometry to challenge sequence-based selections. Reserve one of the four guaranteed slots for the free-loop comparator that performed best in the same structural batch. A new generator would contribute less evidence before the deadline. [Three alternatives and scores](STRATEGIC_OPTIONS.md)

## The contribution we can defend

We tested the model used to choose the team's candidates against a different, measured outcome. Its high-FACS-class AUROC held up at 0.994 on a clonotype split. Across **584** unique, non-WT historical constructs absent from FACS with an interpretable SPR fit status, an untuned BLOSUM62 parent score gave **0.847** versus **0.751** for the frozen model. The paired difference was **+0.095**, with a 95% bootstrap interval of **+0.061 to +0.132**. On 5,798 fit/no-fit pairs matched at the same mutation count, concordance was 0.761 versus 0.577. BLOSUM already has published HER2 precedent. Our contribution is identifying which evidence changes the team's choice, quantifying its limits, and designing the next comparison accordingly. [Measured outcomes and method](RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE.md) · [HER2 source study](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.03.26.586756)

The historical SPR panel was itself partly selected by BLOSUM. Its sampling and related sequences can bias the measured advantage. Just **three** FACS-unseen historical variants have all four anchors, both changed liability-site residues, four to six parent substitutions and no loop cysteine. All three have a reported KD. We cannot estimate discrimination within that exact regime. The eight WT observations span 0.0285–0.178 nM. The competition's same-campaign control determines whether a candidate improves affinity. ## A provisional order for the four guaranteed tests

The team's alternative FACS ensemble scored 0.730 on that same measured fit endpoint. Its nearest-high-FACS distance comparator did as well as BLOSUM overall (0.849 versus 0.847), but gave **exactly the same distance, three, to all 40** enumerated designs. BLOSUM retained 15 distinct scores. This is the strongest reason for the proposed sequence tie-breaker and the clearest concrete example to show judges. The ensemble and this proposal share ten of 20 choices and none of the four leaders. The team's own ranking remains preserved in the late input snapshot. [Comparison of both shortlists and six scores](review/evidence/late_comparison.json)

The team's preliminary multi-seed table at 12:31 UTC had 110 candidate–seed records for 60 sequences, with repeated predictions for 11 and six complete seeds for nine. Recomputing the pooled within-sequence SD on that table gave **0.0147** in minimum VH confidence. Only two WT seeds were represented. All three enumerated leaders here have single-seed changes with absolute magnitude at most 0.014. Slots 1–3 deliberately sample competing rankings. The signs of the predicted differences supply no evidence of superior affinity or even a reliable distinction between their mean structural scores. The ensemble team's 0.872 structural floor is a sensitivity check from those partial data. Four lower-ranked reserves in this proposal (`anas_038`, `anas_003`, `anas_014`, `anas_040`) fall below that floor and warrant review before inclusion in a longer test allocation. | Action | Decision and consequence |
|---|---|
| **KEEP** | Preserve RFD4-Proteina runs, enumeration, the frozen FACS classifier, the full input/output manifests and the matched WT. The original classifier answers a real source-assay question. Retain runs 004/005/007 as evidence of how different generation and selection rules behave. |
| **IMPROVE** | Compare like with like across identical Fab backgrounds, HER2 residues, template, seed and sample. Keep a separate record of sequence identity, physical contact and fit status. Use paired intervals and the three exact-regime examples when explaining the transfer estimate. |
| **REPLACE** | Use the independently measured endpoint to choose the default **sequence** tie-breaker. Replace global ipTM as a gate with explicit VH–HER2 and VL–HER2 evidence, cutoff sensitivity and contemporaneous WT. Inspect disagreement at the individual candidate. | **STOP** | Retire “improved binding”, “preserved epitope” or “improved thermostability” as labels on unmeasured candidates. ## What would change this decision

1. An official full-pair rejection removes that pair from the submission order. Promote the next approved pair that preserves the purpose of the slot where possible. A written resolution of the distance-two boundary could bring the 16 run-010 candidates back into consideration. Ten exceed WT in VH confidence and all 16 sit exactly at that disputed distance. [Completed cross-run comparisons](review/evidence/completed_batches.json)
2. A complete independent-seed batch with consistent chain identities can quantify sensitivity of individual rank contrasts. If a candidate's paired VH advantage changes sign across seeds, mark it unstable and consult the existing sequence and footprint evidence. Pair each seed with its own WT and treat poses from the same sequence as repeated predictions.
3. A blinded, independently selected campaign reversing the SPR ordering would displace BLOSUM as the default. The present findings concern reportability and fitted KD conditional on reportability. Expression yields, competition and thermal measurements can each reverse a design's real-world priority.

The first user is the scientist deciding which Fab constructs to send for expensive follow-up. The first value test after Oslo is a prospective, fixed-budget comparison against their usual score-only choice, using predeclared assay outcomes, equal capacity, exact sequence IDs and the same control. Tabulate expression, fit rates and all three competition outcomes with denominators. Claims about therapeutic efficacy would require additional pharmacology and clinical evidence.
