## What the organisers will evaluate

The 6 November task PDF, pp. 1–4 and 7–9, governs the distinction between the pitch and the lab. Pitch weights are judgment 40%, execution 20%, user fit 20%, path to value 20%. Bring the pitch and GitHub link. Rank up to 20 in advance. Four tests per team are guaranteed, placement adds 12/8/4 and each judge can nominate a team for two extra. The Apheris slides, pp. 6–8, describe 100 tests across 16 teams. Adaptyv uses cell-free expression, HER2 SPR, competition with trastuzumab, and a direct thermal measurement. The experimentally measured reference in that campaign sets the baseline. Teams rank by S-, then A-, then B-tier counts, separately by track. A competitive readout operationalises epitope preservation. The numerical competition and thermal cutoffs, biological replicate plan and fit-censoring limits are unspecified in the supplied material. [Official event FAQ](https://oslo-ai-antibodies-hackathon.vercel.app/)

The official validator strips tags/linkers, uses immunum numbering, assigns the track by type-aware database comparison, and rejects failures. The format must be VHH, scFv or paired Fab-variable chains, numbering confidence ≥0.1, total antibody length ≤250 aa and no unpaired cysteines. De novo requires <70% identity to the closest known antibody and at least two CDRH3 changes from trastuzumab. Optimisation has an additional parent CDRH3 identity and provided-data distance test. Task p. 2 says at least two differences from provided antibodies, while Fig. 2 and p. 7 say **strictly more than two**, with **<70%** parent identity. Slide 6 uses ≥2 and ≤70%. For this ten-mer, four substitutions give 60% identity. The team's local margin of distance ≥3 and ≤60% is a sensible screen. ## Control and input compatibility

* **Antibody.** A is the 107-aa light variable chain and B the 120-aa heavy variable chain. Together they make a 227-aa paired-variable submission. The 336 residues in the modeling input include the antigen. Run 006 WT matches both chains in the supplied SPR reference exactly. Each modeled variant differs from that reference only within B99–108, and its output coordinates reproduce its input sequence. The 11-letter string in the 003 note includes conserved Y109. Eligibility comparisons use the organiser's ten-residue CDRH3.
* **Antigen.** The official YAML and coordinate file split one HER2 domain IV segment into C489–580 (92 aa) and D591–607 (17 aa) around unresolved 581–590. These are fragments of one antigen. An unguided two-chain representation can move them relative to each other. Run 003 encodes their sequences as separate proteins without residue-ID maps or a template. The Boltz inputs retain the author IDs and share a template within run 006. Preserve this distinction when comparing platforms. [Experimental 1N8Z entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1N8Z)
* **JapanFold.** The published PredictRequest/Target schema supports the JSON envelope and multiple protein chains under the 1,536-residue limit. The stored parameters are `output_format=pdb` and `write_pae=true`. These are unguided queries. Explicit `movable_chains` pertains to guided docking. The meaning of this particular split in an unguided job remains undocumented. A structurally different antigen or guided epitope changes the question. AF2-IG's supplied target/binder contract describes two named chains. The documented ligand affinity head is inappropriate for Fab–HER2 KD. [Prediction contract](https://japanfold.aiand.com/docs/predictions/) · [Model limits](https://japanfold.aiand.com/docs/models-and-limits/)
* **Cross-run controls.** Boltz-2 v1.3.0, seed 42, one diffusion sample, three recycling steps and 200 sampling steps in 006. Runs 004/006 share a byte-identical template. Run 001's template is different. Baseline and 004/006 omit B99–108 coordinates. Runs 005/007 supply four anchor coordinates within the loop. None provides a free test of how those four positions move across template conditions. Repeated controls at the same seed establish reproducibility at those settings. They cannot estimate seed variance. [Run receipts](review/evidence/structure_checks.json)
* **Metric attribution.** Foundry's scored exports assign B as binder and `A;C;D` as target. Accordingly global ipTM and minimum interface PAE can include antibody-internal contacts. A minimum over many residue pairs can also be dominated by one favorable pair. The blank `pass` is explained by the recorded absence of enough absolute, group-0 target anchors for target-aligned RMSD. Treat the value as missing. The review verifies query, coordinate and chain order, symmetrizes each directional ipTM pair, keeps A:C, A:D, B:C and B:D separately and reports the minimum of the two VH pairs and equal-weight Fv mean as sensitivity summaries. Equal weight for 92- and 17-residue fragments is a convention. [Source and implementation](review/structure_checks.py)

## Measured data, predictions and unsupported inferences

The 768 historical SPR rows comprise 190 quantified, 460 without reportable KD, 104 inconclusive and 14 unreported. The eight exact WT pairs span 0.028518–0.178 nM, median 0.1345 nM. The 0.152 nM value is real and is the first observation. The 6.24-fold span describes historical measurements under incompletely specified conditions. It supplies neither an error bar for the new experiment nor a required fold improvement. For a binding comparison, use Adaptyv's contemporaneous control and the assay's uncertainty. [Measured-input audit](review/evidence/assay_transfer.json)

The 524,346 labels are yeast-display FACS classes. The saved three-class GBM caps WT near 0.998. The team's earlier random-split AUROC of 0.993 describes high-class discrimination. The separate Mason model trains on **23,834 examples, including 6,254 positive labels**. Its reported AUROC of 0.927 and correlation between its outputs and conservation in a handful of selected designs are exploratory. Tuning a generator and a classifier on the same FACS source creates shared evidence. Selecting 40 from millions by that score forces a narrow high-score band (0.990919–0.996472). [Team training report](people/anas/facs_model_report.txt) · [Fresh split comparison](review/evidence/split_check.json)

The historical row-weighted unseen SPR result (AUC 0.764, fitted-only Spearman −0.430) includes eight WT observations. Excluding that control, keeping each construct once and leaving inconclusive/unreported outcomes outside the fit task gives 0.751 and −0.339. The fixed BLOSUM62 baseline gives 0.847 and −0.496 on the identical constructs. Reportability depends on expression, concentration range and fitting behavior. The SPR design panel arose from multiple methods, including BLOSUM. Public source data, selection bias and sequence relatedness prevent interpreting these results as an independent prospective validation. [Full paired analysis](RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE.md)

At 4.5 Å the crystallographic reference contains 19 contacted HER2 residues and four loop positions. Run 006 WT recovers the 19/19 footprint at that cutoff. All 40 enumerated designs have the four specified amino-acid identities, 28/40 have all four positions physically touching antigen in the refold, and 14/40 have the exact 19-residue antigen footprint with no extras. A free-loop comparator with only two of the four *reference identities* recovers all 19 HER2 footprint residues too. Footprint agreement, identity conservation and competitive binding describe three different observations. The WT crystal itself has two loop contacts at 4.0 Å and four at 4.5/5.0 Å. The predicted candidate counts with all four physical contacts are 25/28/28 at those cutoffs. [Coordinate comparison](review/evidence/structure_checks.json)
