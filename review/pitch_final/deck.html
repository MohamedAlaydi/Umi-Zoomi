Assay predictions

Loop A has the lowest predicted KD. Pick 03 has the highest screening score.

Framework changes

Picks 02 and 04 carry S85N/R87T. The assay models do not score those changes. Their stability effect is unmeasured.

Structural check

All four reach the known site in Mol1. Interface PAE describes model uncertainty; it does not measure affinity.

Held-out test

180 measured constructs. Related sequences were held out together.

Rank correlation: 0.68

Large errors

The model orders some of the affinity range, but errors are large. WT calibration is poor near strong binders.

What this supports

A model-guided experiment list. It does not establish that a new design binds better than trastuzumab.
